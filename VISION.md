# Merrin Field

Merrin Field is a continually changing typographic artwork representing a life through words, imagery, sound, space, and time.

## M0 artistic gate

> Open it and feel that you have entered someone's personal space, not opened a website.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. The work occupies an effectively infinite field rather than a conventional page.
2. There is no required reading direction.
3. Words may be read, seen, shaped, fragmented, layered, or used to form imagery.
4. Typography, image, sound, space, and time belong to the same composition.
5. A change may rearrange the artwork; updates are not required to behave like posts or append-only content.

## What it is not

Merrin Field is not a portfolio, blog, dashboard, social feed, conventional digital garden, or automated model that claims to know what matters in a person's life.

## Artistic authority

Composition is authored. Rules, algorithms, physics, and generative behaviours may be used as artistic tools, but they do not determine meaning or importance.

## First milestone — A Personal Space Exists

The first milestone is reached when a visitor can enter the field, move through it, encounter typographic imagery, readable life material, image, and sound, and feel that they have entered a specific person's space rather than opened a website or technical demo.
